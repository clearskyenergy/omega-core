## Honest constraints to keep
- Nothing is priced in the browser; the engine only charges a PACKAGED
  workspace. A legacy plan's opt-in is a recorded, priced request; ClearSky
  moves the workspace onto a package (admin Package tab) and the invoice
  carries it. Production `GET /api/offerings` reports
  `signup {packaged:false, payNow:false}`: packaged billing flags are off.
- Card data is never entered on our pages: Stripe Customer Portal or
  QuickBooks' invoice payment page.
- Showing a link is never access.
