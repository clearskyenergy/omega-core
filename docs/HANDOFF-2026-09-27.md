     clickable.

     subscription, what you owe and when, the payment method (Stripe
     Customer Portal for a Stripe plan; QuickBooks' own payment page, with
     Save this card + Autopay, for a QuickBooks plan; card data never
     touches our pages), additions waiting, billing history.
     `GET /api/plan-change` is now readable by any verified member;
     changes stay owner/admin.
  5. **Opt in on a legacy plan is priced and recorded**:
     `POST /api/plan-change {action:'opt-in', add}` prices from the book,
     writes `billing/current.optIns[key]` (+ history + admin_audit), mails
     ClearSky (`mail.templates.optInAlert`). Refuses a packaged workspace
     (use the menu) and a workspace with no billing record. Nothing is
     charged.
  6. **Admin tenant Package tab preselects what the tenant has**
     (`admin/package-panel.js` `standing()`): packaged → what it
     subscribes to; legacy → the modules its tier opens by the shared rule
     (`OmegaWorkspaceHub.moduleState`) plus opt-in requests; a "Today"
     strip says tier, holds, pays, requested.
  7. **Editor (Site Map) legibility**: light mode keeps a white top, puts a
     grey ground under it (`--bg:#E9ECEF`), gives each ribbon button a face
     and ink label (tokens `--rbtn-face/-edge/-label/-hover`), darker icons;
     Settings tab › **Appearance**: Light · Dark · Auto
     (`OmegaUI.theme`, stored in `omega.ui.theme`).
