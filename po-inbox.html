Enter many POs at once

One line per product per PO: PO number, SKU, qty, ship-to name, address, city, state, ZIP, requested date (YYYY-MM-DD), notes. Paste rows straight from Excel or Google Sheets (a header row is skipped), or type them with commas; an address with a comma in it goes in "double quotes". Lines with the same PO number are one purchase order going to one place. Each becomes an order awaiting pricing; nothing is accepted or charged.
