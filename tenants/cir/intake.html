Referral intake

Log a referral

A project one of your customers has asked you to work on. Record it here and it enters your service queue, where it can be scoped, priced, and pushed into the editor to be drawn.

Who it's for

Your customer, not you. This is the name the job is tracked and delivered under.
Type freely. Names already in your queue autocomplete, so the same customer doesn't end up spelled three ways.
Optional. Where the work came from, if it's worth knowing.

The project

Only the name is required. Everything else can follow — a referral with a name and nothing else is still a referral, and blocking on detail you don't have yet is how work goes unlogged.
Blank reads as not stated, which is honest. Zero would read as a project with no size, which isn't.

What they've asked you to do

Your service lines. Pick every one this job touches — the queue counts a referral once per line, which is how the load per service becomes readable.
Not sure yet? Leave them all unticked. The referral lands under Needs scoping on the queue rather than being filed as a job of no size.

Priority and timing

Priority sets the reply target the queue measures you against, and the derived due date when no date is given.
Optional. Left blank, the queue derives one from priority and marks it with an asterisk so nobody mistakes it for a commitment the customer made.

Files

Site plans, one-lines, utility correspondence, an interconnection letter — anything the job starts from.
