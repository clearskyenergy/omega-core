Sites & custody

Every shipping unit after it leaves the plant: who holds it, which end site it is bound to, and the warranty or SLA that binds. A move the rules refuse is refused with the reason; nothing here is inferred.

Add a site

Many sites at once

A customer's PO often lists its sites in an email or a sheet. Paste the list here to add every address as a site on the customer's account. Then spread the order's units over those sites, lowest serial first. Each unit is then going to its site, and receiving it binds it there. Nothing is saved until you press Create or Assign; the preview shows exactly what will change. To send a few units to one site, use Going to in the Fleet register.

1 · Paste the list

One address per line, Street, City, ST ZIP, as it came in the email. Bullets and numbering are fine. To name a site, put the name first with a colon: Store 12: 100 Main St, …. To set its units, add a count at the end: x4 or (4 units). A sheet with a header row also works (name, address, city, state, zip, units, store #).

3 · Assign the order's units

Tick the sites the order goes to and enter how many units each one takes. A blank box takes an even share of the rest (the grey number), and when the units don't divide evenly the first sites get one extra. Preview lists the serials for each site before anything is saved. One per site puts 1 in every ticked box: each site gets one unit, and the rest of the order waits for the next list.

Scan session

A gun types the serial and presses Enter; the camera reads a QR or Code 128. Receive a load checks each scan against the load Shipping planned and names shorts and overages. Assign to a site binds each scanned unit to the site chosen below.

Import site assignments

A spreadsheet from the customer or the installer: one row per serial with the site, its address and the dates. Columns are matched by name, you confirm the mapping, the plan is shown row by row, and nothing is written until you commit. A second run of the same sheet changes nothing. Download the template.

Exceptions

What loses a warranty claim: commissioned with no site, received and never assigned, stuck in transit, damaged or quarantined, coverage about to end.

Coverage templates

Per product: a warranty or SLA term and what starts it. A unit's coverage is worked out from these and its custody dates every time it is shown, so a change here applies to every unit of the product. With no template, warranty years on the product counts from the ship date.
