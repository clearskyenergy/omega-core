Your own numbers, per supplier. Nothing here ships with a price — a figure appears because somebody put a real quote behind it, and the estimate says which supplier and which quote it used. Leave a field empty and the estimate falls back to its generic rate and tells you so.

A quote file is one supplier's prices and the scope behind them, entered once and shared with the team. It stays on this machine until you press Save to my organisation, which writes it to your organisation's own record — not to anywhere public.
