Before you start

Omega Logic is one app for running your business: sales, customers, the plant, deliveries, stock and money. ClearSky makes it. Your company is the workspace inside it, so everyone at your company sees the same orders and the same numbers.

What you need

  • ✓

    Your work account. Google with your company email, or your work email and a password, like you@yourcompany.com. Not a personal Gmail.

  • ✓

    A place on your company's workspace. Whoever runs Omega Logic at your company adds you. If your company isn't on Omega Logic yet, ClearSky sets it up.

  • ✓

    A phone, tablet or computer. iPhone or iPad with Safari, an Android phone with Chrome, or any computer with Chrome, Edge or Safari.

  • ✓

    Nothing from an app store. You add it straight from the web page. It's free to install, and it updates itself every time you open it.

Using it · Home

Home: the hex hub

Home asks Where to? Seven hexagons, one for each part of the business, drawn from the Omega Logic logo. A red number is how many things there need a person.

  • ◷

    Today, in the middle. Everything that needs a person today: customer requests, orders to price, POs to review, follow-ups that are due. Tap it to jump to the list.

  • ▤

    Sales. Orders to price, customer requests to answer, company POs to review.

  • ◉

    Customers. Every customer account (page 8). The number is follow-ups due and people asking to join.

  • ⚙

    Plant. Work orders: late, on hold, in build.

  • ◎

    Deliver. Units ready to ship, units a customer placed at a site for you to confirm, loads on the road.

  • ▦

    Stock. Parts short for the open work.

  • $

    Money. Invoices to issue, overdue and open, each with its pay link.

Tap a hexagon, its panel opens

Under the hub: first the live list (tap a line to open it), then Work it, every screen for that part of the business. Tap another hexagon to switch. Below the panels, Home lists what needs a person, then money and the plant.

Using it · Customers

Customers: the whole account in one place

Like the customer hub in QuickBooks. Tap Customers, pick a company, and everything about them is on one screen, in sections across the top. A customer is a company account with its people on it.

The sections

Overview
Invoiced, received, the balance and open requests. Quick buttons: Log a call, Add a contact, Upload a document, Enter a PO. Their app: the link to send them (page 10).
People
Who signs in to their app: Approve someone who asked to join, Turn off someone who left, Invite, or add a person. Plus contacts who never log in: site leads, accounts payable, engineers.
Activity
Log a call, email, meeting, note or task. Put a date in Follow up on and it shows on Today that day, until you mark it done.
Documents
A contract, a drawing, a datasheet, a photo from your phone (up to 2 MB). Tick Share with the customer and they see it in their app; leave it unticked and it stays in your office. What they upload lands here too.
Orders
Every order and PO on the account. Enter a PO for them: it becomes an order waiting for a price. Nothing is accepted or charged.
Sites
Their sites and the units at each. Tap a unit for its passport.
Timeline
All of the above, newest first.
Using it · The tabs

Orders, Sites and the Menu

Orders

Every order with its customer, stage, total and balance. Open one for its lines, money, build and shipment, and the customer's requests: type the answer and tap Answer & close. If your company invoices on its own paper, mark each invoice Issued and Received here.

Sites

The customer says: each unit your customer placed at a site; tap Confirm once it's checked. Receive a load: pick the load, then scan or type each serial (Camera scans). Unit passport: type a serial to see where it is, its warranty and its history.

Menu

Every screen in one place, with a search box. The ones you use most are along the top. Then one panel per hub, Setup (products, settings) and Help & guides, where this guide lives.

What stays on the desktop, on purpose

Approving a price, accepting an order, recording a shipment and settling a wire happen in the desktop office (silmarillion.clearskyomega.com/omega-logic, page 5), next to the paperwork. The app shows every one of those results the moment they're saved, and never makes up a number.

Your customers

Your customers' app

Your customers don't get Omega Logic. They get your company's app, with your name and your icon, and everything they do in it lands in your Customers hub.

  • Send it from their account: Customers, the company, Overview, then Their app. Send the app link opens your phone's share sheet, so you can text or email it. Desktop link is for a computer. Under People, Invite emails a person the link.
  • They install it the same way you did: Safari, the menu or Share, Add to Home Screen on an iPhone; Chrome, , Install on Android.
  • In it they can size and design a site, send or upload POs, pay invoices, follow shipping, and record where each unit went, with its warranty.
  • You see it all: a PO becomes an order waiting for a price, a question shows on Today, a document lands in Documents.

Their guide is under Menu → Help & guides → Customer app guide. Send it with the link.

If something looks wrong

No Add to Home Screen, no InstallThe link opened inside another app (Gmail, Outlook, Teams, Slack, LinkedIn, WhatsApp) or a Private tab. Open it in Safari on an iPhone, Chrome on Android. iPhone: swipe up on the share sheet; still missing, Edit Actions at the bottom.
The icon is a letter or an old pictureRemove it (touch and hold the icon, Delete Bookmark or Remove App, then Delete) and add it again from Safari. Android: touch and hold, Uninstall, then install again.
It looks out of dateUpdates arrive by themselves: the app loads the newest version whenever it opens online. Close it fully and open it again, or tap .
No signalThe app says it's offline and shows nothing, rather than yesterday's list. Reconnect and tap .
"Nowhere to go yet"You signed in with the wrong account, or nobody has added you yet. See page 6.
A button says noThe message says why: a unit can't be commissioned before it has been received, for example. Do the earlier step first. The rules are the same on every screen.
Removing itiPhone: touch and hold the icon, Delete Bookmark (or Remove App), then Delete; no delete option means Screen Time is blocking it. Android: touch and hold, Uninstall. Chrome or Edge: in the app's window, open its menu ( or ), Uninstall Omega Logic…, then Remove. Safari on a Mac: in Finder, open the Applications folder in your home folder and drag Omega Logic to the Trash. Your data stays in the workspace.
