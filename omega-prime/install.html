Install Omega Prime

The guided site flow, on your home screen. One site: the grid, the circuit, the system, and the quote.

Scan with the phone's camera. It opens this page.

iPhone · Safari

  1. Open this page in Safari (Chrome on iPhone cannot add it).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

The icon is Omega Prime. Opening it starts the guided flow.

Android · Chrome

In Chrome, tap the menu (⋮) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Omega Prime is installed. Open it from your home screen.
