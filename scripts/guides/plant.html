2. Put it on your phone

It is a web app: nothing to download from a store, and every update reaches the phone the next time it is opened. Open the address once from the link the office sends; the app remembers the workspace after that. Sign in with the work Google account that is a member of the workspace.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Safari and go to the link your office sent.
  2. Tap the menu button at the left of the address bar (iOS 27) or ··· at the right (iOS 26), then Share. See a Share button (the square with the arrow)? Just tap that.
  3. Swipe up on the sheet or tap View More, then Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App on and tap Add. No switch? Older iOS: just tap Add.
  5. Open it from the Home Screen from now on and sign in there: it keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari. It opens full screen as Omega Logic, ClearSky's app, with your company's workspace shown inside.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome and go to the link your office sent.
  2. Tap ⋮ at the right of the address bar, then Install and create shortcut (older Chrome: Install app or Add to Home screen).
  3. Tap Install, not Create shortcut.
  4. Open it from its icon, with your other apps, and sign in.

3. The five tabs

  • Today: open work orders, late, with holds, mine; today's scans, units that reached Ready, refusals, test fails; needs a person (held, stuck, failed, quiet benches, tap to open); Scan at a bench.
  • Work: every open work order, late first, with All / Mine / Late / Holds / Ready filters. Tap one: stage, target, built %, ready units, holds, next operation, what it is short of, every unit with its station and step progress.
  • Scan: opens the bench screen. Paired once as a roaming phone, the builder picks the bench they are at on each scan. Look up any serial from here.
  • Stock: finished units by product (available, assigned, building) and the parts short for the open work.
  • Quality: units on hold. Open one to place a hold with the reason, or release it with the disposition. A hold stops every scan on that unit until it is released. Once a unit has shipped, its record shows After the plant: where it is now, which site it went to, and a link to its custody passport.

The bench

Scan the unit's serial or its label. The screen shows the steps for this bench with each part and quantity for this unit. Scan a part's label, or tap Issue, to take it off stock and onto the unit; a lot number travels with it. A check step gets Done. When every step is complete the bench says which bench is next; the next bench refuses the unit while anything here is open. Scans queue when the yard has no signal and send when it returns.

4. How it links to the main system

  1. The office accepts an order on the desktop; when the deposit is paid it is released and a work order exists. It appears on the Plant app's Today and Work tabs.
  2. Serials are registered on the desktop plant board. The builders build bench by bench with the scanner, issuing parts as they go. The materials plan nets what was issued; Stock in the Omega Logic app shows what is short.
  3. A unit that reaches Ready is finished stock. The office assigns it to an order from the Omega Logic app, or the plant built it for one already.
  4. The office records the shipment on the desktop. From then on the unit's custody chain starts: in transit, received, assigned to the customer's site, commissioned. The Plant app shows that under After the plant on the unit, and the customer sees it on their phone.
  5. Every unit's full record — the plant trace, the tests, the scans, the custody chain and the warranty — is one passport on the desktop: silmarillion.clearskyomega.com/logic-custody?org=yourcompany.com.

5. If something looks wrong

  • "Open the app once from your office link so it knows your workspace": open the Plant app link your office sent once (it ends in ?org= and your company's email domain); the app remembers it.
  • The app looks stale: close it fully and open it again. Updates arrive on the next open; data is never shown from a cache.
  • Offline: the app says so and shows nothing rather than yesterday's list.
  • Questions: ClearSky Energy Solutions. The full manual is in the workspace documentation.
  • Nothing loads after sign-in: your email is not a member of the workspace yet. Contact ClearSky or your administrator.
  • The bench says it is not paired: a plant administrator creates a Roaming phone credential under Work orders on the desktop; paste the token once on the bench screen.
