**FCC:** No nationwide availability or Location Fabric records included. The importer processes files you are authorized to use. An exact location-ID join is required for geospatial availability analysis. Protect coordinates and source records according to their applicable terms; do not infer routes from location points or H3 cells.

**Public ArcGIS copies:** The unattributed national long-haul item and the old Zayo customer-map copy were cataloged but excluded. Public reachability did not establish the publisher, currency or rights to republish.

The package contains no secrets, customer-site records, restricted Fabric data or copied PeeringDB bulk records. Synthetic test records are confined to `tests/`; they are never added to the map datasets.
