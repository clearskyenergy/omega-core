Your account on your phone

Your company's account with your supplier: site plans in Editor Lite, the account's orders with their milestone and warranty, purchase orders, and Sites & equipment — where each unit is going, when it arrived, which site it runs at. Everyone at your company on the account sees the same.

The app wears your supplier's name and icon. The app and the desktop portal are the same account. What you do on the phone — a purchase order, a question on an order, where a battery is going — is what your supplier sees the moment it is saved.

2. Put it on your phone

It is a web app: nothing to download from a store, and every update reaches the phone the next time it is opened. Open the link your supplier sent once; the app remembers your supplier after that. Tap Email my login link, open the email and tap the link, or sign in with Google with your work email.

Your company's account. If your company already has an account with this supplier, signing in from your company email asks to join it: the account's owner (or your supplier) approves you, and then you see everything on it. The owner can also add you first under Account → People on this account. If your company has no account yet, the first sign-in opens one and you are its owner.

iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open Safari and go to the address above.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow, at the bottom of the screen).
  4. Scroll the sheet and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  5. Open it from the home screen from now on. It opens full screen, with the company's icon and name.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open Chrome and go to the address above.
  2. Sign in.
  3. Tap the Chrome menu (three dots) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm. The app appears with your other apps, with its own icon.

3. The four tabs

  • Design: Site Map · Editor Lite — a new site plan from an address, a quick size (kW and hours against your supplier's catalog) with Lay it out and Send as a PO, and every saved plan.
  • Orders: each order with its milestone (received, confirmed, building, testing, ready, shipped), lines with the warranty date, invoices with the pay link, destinations and loads, and Request a change or ask a question.
  • POs: paste your purchase orders, one line per product per PO, and Send these purchase orders. Each is received for pricing; nothing is charged.
  • Account: your account number, company, rep and terms; People on this account (the owner adds colleagues, approves anyone who asked to join, and turns access off for someone who left); your details; and Sites & equipment.

Sites & equipment — "this one is going here"

  1. Add a site: the location where the units will be interconnected — address, utility, meter, point of interconnection. Once.
  2. While a unit is in transit, choose the site under Going to. When it arrives, tap Received: it is placed at that site.
  3. Or tap Received first and then Assign it to the site it is installed at.
  4. Tap Commissioned with the date and who did it.
  5. Each unit shows its warranty (from, until) and any service level. The warranty is tied to the site. Until your supplier confirms your placement it reads awaiting your supplier's confirmation; then confirmed by your supplier with the date.

4. How it reaches your supplier

  1. A PO anyone on the account sends from the app lands with your supplier as an order to price. Everyone on the account sees it under Orders once it is confirmed.
  2. Your supplier accepts and invoices on their desktop; the milestone on your order moves as their plant scans your units — nobody types a status.
  3. When your units ship, each one appears under Sites & equipment with its serial. You say where it is going and when it arrived; you assign it to the site it runs at; you record commissioning.
  4. Your supplier sees exactly what you said, on their phone and in their office, and confirms it. From then on the unit's warranty and any service level are on record against that site, and a replacement under RMA carries the remaining term.
  5. A question, a delivery change or a warranty claim goes in under the order (Request a change or ask a question); the answer comes back on the same order.
One account, one record. The app, the desktop portal and your supplier all read the same record, and so does everyone on your company's account. Nothing is stored only on the phone.

5. If something looks wrong

  • "This link is missing its supplier": open the app from the link your supplier sent once; the app remembers the supplier after that.
  • The app looks stale: close it fully and open it again. Updates arrive on the next open; data is never shown from a cache.
  • Offline: the app says so and shows nothing rather than yesterday's list.
  • Questions: your supplier — your account rep is on the Account tab.
  • "Your request to join … is waiting for approval": your company already has an account; its owner or your supplier lets you in.
  • "Open your customer account first": tap Account once, or ask your company's account owner to add you.
  • A serial is "not on one of your orders": the unit was shipped on an order for a different company account. Ask your supplier which account it is on.
  • Editor Lite on a phone: it opens in the browser at desktop width; it lays out best on a tablet or a desktop.
