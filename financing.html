Apply for financing

Goes to DLL as a pricing request. Everything below mirrors their intake sheet. Save a draft as often as you like — once submitted it is final on your side, and ClearSky takes it from there.

Request

Indicative gives a ballpark. Executable is a quotable number and needs the credit package below.

Customer

The legal entity that would sign, not the trading name.

Project

Do not subtract ITC or incentives from the financed amount. DLL finances the gross project cost — netting the credit down here understates the request and the pricing comes back wrong.

ITC & depreciation

Whether these are retained by the customer or by DLL is part of what is being priced. Estimates are fine; say so if they are rough.

Timing

Credit package

Under $250k and auto-approved needs no documents. Over $250k, DLL asks for all of the below.

  • Project scope / proposal
  • Company mission
  • Additional supporting info
  • Credit: 2 years financials (P&L and balance sheet) — or 2 years audited — or 2 years tax returns
  • Plus YTD interim financials
