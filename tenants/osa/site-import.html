Review inbox

Imported sites are not part of any portfolio number, not shareable and not visible to any other portal until someone here accepts them. Rejecting needs a reason.

Share accepted sites with another portal, read-only

A share is a grant on the canonical record: the other org reads it and cannot edit or delete it. Nothing is copied. Owner mailing, lease and revenue-share terms are stripped by the server before any partner sees the record. Revoking removes their read immediately.
