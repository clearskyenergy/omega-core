Each customer is a company account with its people on it: the people, the contacts who never log in, what was said and agreed, the documents, every order and site. These are not platform users or plant operators.

Customer sign-in

Customers sign in to the customer app (or the same account on a desktop) with their own verified email. Everyone on an account sees its orders, sites and equipment; someone who signs in from the company's email domain asks to join and the owner or you approve.
