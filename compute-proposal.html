Site & host
City and state are required. Without them a geocoder picks one of the dozens of streets with that name rather than admitting it cannot tell.
Gate 1 · Power
The gate is ~1 MW with a utility will-serve or a clear path to one. Grid Atlas measures how far the substations are; only the utility knows what the feeder has left.
Gate 2 · Fiber — hard gate
1 Gbps bidirectional is the hard gate. A site without it is not a compute site regardless of how good the power is. If there is no service, capture a budgetary quote to bring it in.
Gate 3 · Zoning
Zoning of the exact area where the containers would sit — not the parcel headline and not the neighbour's.
Gate 4 · Site control
Tranche — what will the host give?
These three questions are the whole tranche classification. Until one is answered the offer prices at the Tranche 1 floor — never the premium — so an unasked question can cost us upside but can never over-commit you in the room.
